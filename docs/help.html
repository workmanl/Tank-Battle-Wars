Help and support

Common questions about Tank Battle Wars.

Frequently asked questions

What is included for free, and what does Full Unlock do?

You can play 3 battles every day for free across solo, campaign, and two-player modes, and earn coins by winning matches. Full Unlock is a one-time $2.99 purchase that removes the daily battle limit permanently.

Do I have to buy coins to unlock weapons?

No. The weapon shop is designed around coins earned from playing and winning matches. Optional coin packs ($0.99 for 500, $1.99 for 1,500, or $4.99 for 5,000) are available if you want them, but are not required.

How do I restore my Full Unlock purchase?

Full Unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. On a new device, open the unlock screen in the game and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account. Coins, coin packs and campaign progress are saved only on the device and are not restored if you delete the app.

Can two people play together on one device?

Yes. You can play turn-based duels against a friend on the same iPhone or iPad by passing the device between turns.

Does Tank Battle Wars require an internet connection?

No. All gameplay runs offline on your device. An internet connection is only needed when making an App Store purchase.

Why does the app ask for permission to add to Photos?

Permission is requested only if you choose to save a battle result card image to your photo library. The app can only add the image you ask to save and cannot view or read any existing photos.

How do I get help or report a bug?

Email hello@emrldlabs.com, or open an issue on GitHub at github.com/workmanl/Tank-Battle-Wars/issues.
