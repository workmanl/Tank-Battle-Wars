Tank Battle Wars

Turn-based tank artillery duels with destructible terrain on iOS.

Tank Battle Wars is a side-view turn-based artillery game where you set your firing angle and power, read the wind, and blast craters into the terrain until one tank remains. Play solo against the computer or pass your iPhone or iPad to a friend for a two-player local duel.

Free to download (3 battles per day). Optional one-time Full Unlock for $2.99, plus optional coin packs ($0.99 to $4.99).

What it does

Solo and two-player duels

Face off against computer opponents or play against a friend on the same iPhone or iPad in pass-and-play mode. Each turn, dial in your angle and power to land a direct hit across the battlefield.

Destructible terrain and ballistics

Projectiles follow gravity and shifting wind between turns. Every explosion carves away the ground, letting you dig trenches for cover, collapse ridges, or bury an opponent's tank.

Campaign, biomes, and shop

Battle across multiple terrain biomes in campaign mode and unlock achievements as you play. Winning matches earns coins that you can spend in the weapon shop to expand your arsenal.

Offline play without ads

The game runs completely offline with no internet connection required and no advertisements interrupting matches.

How it works

  1. Start a battle

    Choose a single-player campaign stage, a quick computer match, or a two-player duel on the same device.

  2. Aim and read the wind

    Pick a weapon from your loadout, check the wind speed and direction, and set your turret angle and firing power.

  3. Fire and reshape the ground

    Launch your shot to damage enemy tanks and blast craters into the terrain until only one tank is left standing.

  4. Upgrade in the shop

    Use the coins you earn from victories to unlock new weapons for future battles.
