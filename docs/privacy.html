Privacy policy

Tank Battle Wars, published by Emrld Labs.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Summary

Tank Battle Wars runs offline on your iPhone or iPad and collects no personal data. The app contains no networking code, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no user accounts.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. The developer does not collect any data from this app.

What we collect

None. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

  • Coins, unlocked weapons, and in-game shop purchases
  • Campaign progress and unlocked achievements
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Daily battle counter so the 3 free daily battles reset each day

Permissions

  • Photo Library (Add Only): Requested only when you choose to save a battle result card image to your Photos library. The app cannot read or browse your existing photos.

You can change these permissions at any time in Settings.

Network use

The app contains no networking code (no URLSession). The only network communication is handled by Apple StoreKit when you make an in-app purchase.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Emrld Labs never sees or receives your payment card details or Apple Account credentials.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
